Welcome to the **Computational Atomistic Methods and Machine Learning** group, based at SIMaP in Grenoble.

We develop physics-informed machine learning for materials science: equivariant neural networks that respect the symmetries of nature, and generative models that design new materials and molecules with the properties we need. [Read more about our research →](/research/)
